---

## 第 1 段

| 单词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 原文语境 |
|-----------|------|------|----------|
| **gig workers** | phr. | 零工/打零工者 | gig workers, with middle-income jobs undercut |
| **undercut** | v. | 削弱；使贬值 | middle-income jobs undercut by automation |
| **politically sustainable** | phr. | 政治上可持续 | will not be politically sustainable |
| **boost productivity** | phr. | 提高生产率 | boost productivity in various sectors |

---
