---

## 第 1–2 段

| 单词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 原文语境 |
|-----------|------|------|----------|
| **considerable buzz** | phr. | 广泛关注 | attracting considerable buzz in Silicon Valley |
| **productivity software** | phr. | 生产力软件 | the market for "productivity" software |
| **from the ground up** | phr. | 从零开始 | designed from the ground up for the smartphone world |
| **scrapped** | v. | 废弃 | both apps … were later scrapped |
| **acqui-hire** | n. | 收购人才 | many "acqui-hires" |
| **insatiable hunger** | phr. | 永不满足的需求 | insatiable hunger for tech-talent |

---

## 第 3–4 段

| 单词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 原文语境 |
|-----------|------|------|----------|
| **remorseless drive** | phr. | 无情推进 | a remorseless drive by Big Tech |
| **chew up** | phr. | 吞并 | to chew up any innovative companies |
| **seedling** | n. | 幼苗（喻初创） | They bought the seedlings and closed them down |
| **put paid to** | phr. | 终结 | putting paid to businesses |
| **decline to comment** | phr. | 拒绝置评 | Microsoft declined to comment |
| **mixed feelings** | phr. | 复杂心情 | admits to mixed feelings about the result |
| **selfish hat** | phr. | 自私角度（口语） | if I put my selfish hat on |

## 第 5–7 段

| 单词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 原文语境 |

---

## 易混 / 同义替换

| 原文 | 近义/替换 |
|------|-----------|
| scrapped | discontinued / shelved |
| acqui-hire | talent acquisition via buyout |
| remorseless | relentless / ruthless |
| chew up | absorb / eliminate |
| put paid to | put an end to |
| drop in the ocean | negligible amount |
