# 2011 考研英语（二）Text 2 — 中难点词汇

> 主题：报纸业「已死」预言落空，行业调整与盈利模式变化。

---

## 速查总表

| 词/短语 | 释义 |
|---------|------|
| chronicling | 记录（常指记录衰落） |
| doom | 厄运；末日 |
| subsidize | 补贴 |
| out of date | 过时的 |
| shrug off | 摆脱；不受影响 |
| stay afloat | 维持运转 |
| push overboard | 裁掉（喻推下海） |
| reckon | 估计 |
| have the nerve to | 胆敢 |
| desperate measure |  desperate 措施 |
| reliance on | 依赖 |
| whirlwind | 旋风（喻剧变） |
| distinctive | 有特色的 |
| foreign bureau | 驻外记者站 |
| savagely cut | 大幅削减 |
| completeness | 完整性；面面俱到 |

---

## 第 1 段

| 单词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 原文语境 |
|-----------|------|------|----------|
| **chronicle** | v. | 记录（尤指按时间记载） | chronicling their own doom |
| **doom** | n. | 厄运；毁灭 | their own doom |
| **Federal Trade Commission** | 专名 | 联邦贸易委员会 | America's Federal Trade Commission |
| **charitable corporation** | phr. | 慈善型公司 | become charitable corporations |
| **subsidize** | v. | 补贴 | Should the state subsidize them |
| **out of date** | phr. | 过时的 | discussions now seem out of date |

---

## 第 2 段

| 单词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 原文语境 |
|-----------|------|------|----------|
| **little sign of crisis** | phr. | 几乎没有危机迹象 | little sign of crisis |
| **shrug off** | phr. | 摆脱；不受影响 | have shrugged off the recession |
| **inhabit** | v. | 处于；占据（某处境） | inhabit the most troubled corner |
| **profit margin** | phr. | 利润率 | 20% profit margins |

## 第 3 段

| 单词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 原文语境 |
|-----------|------|------|----------|
| **stay afloat** | phr. | 维持运转；不倒闭 | papers stayed afloat |
| **push … overboard** | phr. | 把……推下海（喻裁员） | pushing journalists overboard |
| **reckon** | v. | 估计；认为 | reckons that 13,500 jobs have gone |
| **newsroom** | n. | 新闻编辑部 | newsroom jobs |
| **slimmer** | adj. | 更精简的 | paying more for slimmer products |
| **have the nerve to** | phr. | 胆敢 | had the nerve to refuse delivery |
| **desperate measure** | phr. | 孤注一掷的措施 | these desperate measures |
| **push further** | phr. | 进一步推进（裁员等） | they can be pushed further |

## 第 5 段

| 单词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 原文语境 |
|-----------|------|------|----------|
| **whirlwind** | n. | 旋风（喻剧烈变革） | The whirlwind that swept through newsrooms |

| **foreign bureau** | phr. | 驻外记者站 | Foreign bureaus have been savagely cut |

## 易混 / 同义替换

| 原文 | 近义/替换 |
|------|-----------|
| chronicling doom | recording decline |
| shrug off recession | recover from recession |
| stay afloat | survive |
| push journalists overboard | lay off journalists |
| reliance on ads | depend on advertising |
| savagely cut | drastically reduce |
