# 2011 考研英语（二）Text 1 — 中难点词汇

> 主题：独立董事（outside directors）与公司治理、声誉风险。

---

| 词/短语 | 释义 |
|---------|------|
| outside director | 独立董事 |
| under fire | 受到严厉批评 |
| compensation committee | 薪酬委员会 |
| pass unremarked | 未加质疑地通过 |
| biased | 有偏见的 |
| presumably | 据推测 |
| weather a crisis | 渡过危机 |
| proxy statement | 委托书；股东委托书 |

---

## 第 1 段

| 单词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 原文语境 |
|-----------|------|------|----------|
| **outside director** | n. | 独立董事 | joined… as an outside director |
| **attract criticism** | phr. | 引来批评 | without attracting much criticism |
| **under fire** | phr. | 受到猛烈批评 | was under fire for having sat on… |
| **compensation committee** | phr. | 薪酬委员会 | Goldman's compensation committee |
| **payout** | n. | 支付款；奖金支出 | enormous bonus payouts |
| **pass unremarked** | phr. | 未受注意地通过；未加反对 | let those payouts pass unremarked |

---

## 第 2 段

| 单词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 原文语境 |
|-----------|------|------|----------|
| **biased** | adj. | 有偏见的 | less biased advisers |
| **presumably** | adv. | 据推测；大概 | they presumably have enough independence |
| **disagree with** | phr. | 反对（提议） | disagree with the chief executive's proposals |
| **weather** | v. | 经受住；渡过 | having weathered their own crises |

---

## 第 3 段

| 单词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 原文语境 |
|-----------|------|------|----------|
| **database** | n. | 数据库 | used a database that covered… |
| **proxy statement** | phr. | 股东委托书 | from one proxy statement to the next |
| **depart** | v. | 离开（职位） | reason for departing a board |
| **concentrate on** | phr. | 集中研究 | concentrated on those "surprise" disappearances |
| **restate earnings** | phr. | 重述收益（修正财报） | have to restate earnings |
| **class-action lawsuit** | phr. | 集体诉讼 | named in a federal class-action lawsuit |
| **perform worse** | phr. | 表现更差 | the stock is likely to perform worse |
| **correlation** | n. | 相关性 | a correlation between them leaving and… |
| **suggestive** | adj. | 暗示性的 | is suggestive, it does not mean… |
| **jump off a sinking ship** | phr. | 弃船而逃（喻董事避险离职） | jumping off a sinking ship |
| **trade up** | phr. | 跳槽升级 | Often they "trade up" |

## 第 4 段

| 单词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 原文语境 |
|-----------|------|------|----------|
| **blow to reputation** | phr. | 声誉打击 | avoiding a blow to their reputations |

---

## 易混 / 同义替换

| 原文 | 近义/替换 |
|------|-----------|
| under fire | under attack / criticized |
| pass unremarked | go unnoticed / without objection |
| restate earnings | revise financial statements |
| suggestive | indicative / implying |
| trade up | move to a better position |
| weather crises | survive crises |
