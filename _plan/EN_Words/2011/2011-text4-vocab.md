## 易混 / 同义替换

| 原文 | 近义/替换 |
|------|-----------|
| chronic vs acute | long-term vs severe/urgent |
| converge | come together / align |
| disintegration | breakup / collapse |
| harmonization | coordination / integration |
| devaluation | currency depreciation |
| write off | dismiss as finished |
| blunt the edges | soften the impact |
