## 第 1–2 段

| 单词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 原文语境 |
|-----------|------|------|----------|
| **labor shortage** | phr. | 劳动力短缺 | complaining of labor shortages |
| **overhaul** | n./v. | 彻底改革 | without an overhaul of immigration rules |
| **straightforward** | adj. | 简明的 | a more straightforward agricultural-workers visa |
| **undocumented** | adj. | 无合法身份的 | undocumented immigrants |
| **pickup** | n. | 回升 | a similarly sustained pickup in the job market |

## 易混 / 同义替换

| 原文 | 近义/替换 |
|------|-----------|
| overhaul | reform / revamp |
| undocumented | illegal / without papers |
| implausible | unlikely / unrealistic |
| labor-intensive | requiring much manual work |
| cumbersome | burdensome / complicated |
| compounded | worsened / intensified |
