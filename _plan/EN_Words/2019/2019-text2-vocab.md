## 第 1–2 段

| 单词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 原文语境 |
|-----------|------|------|----------|
| **soak up** | phr. | 吸收 | forests to soak up … carbon dioxide |
| **hasten** | v. | 加速 | The climate change we are hastening |
| **emit** | v. | 排放 | forests that emit more carbon than they absorb |
| **carbon sink** | phr. | 碳汇 | valuable "carbon sinks" |
| **sequester** | v. | 封存 | capacity to sequester carbon |
| **strike a balance** | phr. | 取得平衡 | involves striking a subtle balance |

---

## 第 3 段

| 单词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 原文语境 |
|-----------|------|------|----------|
| **thin out** | phr. | 疏伐 | thin out young trees |
| **clear brush** | phr. | 清理灌木 | clear brush in parts of the forest |
| **controlled burning** | phr. | 可控焚烧 | including by controlled burning |
| **carbon-carrying capacity** | phr. | 载碳能力 | temporarily lowers carbon-carrying capacity |
| **draw moisture** | phr. | 吸收水分 | draw a greater share of the available moisture |
| **fend off** | phr. | 抵御 | better able to fend off bark beetles |
| **combustible** | adj. | 易燃的 | rendered less combustible |
| **scorch** | v. | 烧焦 | wildfires have scorched hundreds of thousands of acres |

## 易混 / 同义替换

| 原文 | 近义/替换 |
|------|-----------|
| soak up | absorb / take in |
| sequester | store / lock away |
| thin out | selectively cut |
| fend off | ward off / resist |
| combustible | flammable |
| prioritize | rank / focus on first |
