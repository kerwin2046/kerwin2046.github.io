## 第 1 段

| 单词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 原文语境 |
|-----------|------|------|----------|
| **emerge** | v. | 出现；浮现 | guilt emerges a little later |
| **in conjunction with** | phr. | 与……同时 | in conjunction with a child's growing grasp |
| **grasp** | n./v. | 理解；掌握 | growing grasp of social and moral norms |
| **appease** | v. | 安抚 | such statements appease parents and friends |
| **conscience** | n. | 良心 | their own consciences |
| **moral guilt** | phr. | 道德内疚 | so-called moral guilt |
| **reparative** | adj. | 弥补性的 | behavior that's not only reparative |
| **prosocial** | adj. | 亲社会的 | but also prosocial |
