## 第 1–3 段

| 单词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 原文语境 |
|-----------|------|------|----------|
| **swap out** | phr. | 换掉 | swap out your single-use plastic staples |
| **single-use** | adj. | 一次性的 | curb single-use plastics |
| **overarching** | adj. | 总体的 | the overarching message is directed at individuals |
| **lead with wallets** | phr. | 用钱投票（消费主义） | Lead with your wallets |
| **perpetuate** | v. | 使延续 | perpetuating this individualistic narrative |
| **blight** | v. | 破坏；玷污 | Our oceans are blighted by the stuff |
| **dismiss** | v. | 不予重视 | I'm not dismissing individual actions |

## 第 4–6 段

|-----------|------|------|----------|
| **plastics footprint** | phr. | 塑料足迹 | minimize my own plastics footprint |
| **packaging-free** | adj. | 无包装的 | buying packaging-free food |

| **bulk store** | phr. | 散装店 | going to a bulk store |

## 第 7–8 段

| 单词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 原文语境 |
|-----------|------|------|----------|
| **moral licensing** | phr. | 道德许可 | a kind of "moral licensing" |
| **detrimental** | adj. | 有害的 | They could even be detrimental |

| **systemic change** | phr. | 系统性变革 | push for real systemic change |
| **hold to account** | phr. | 问责 | hold our governments and industries to account |
| **shop sustainably** | phr. | 可持续消费 | we must shop sustainably |
| **citizen** | n. | 公民（对比 consumer） | rather than as "citizens" |

## 易混 / 同义替换

| 原文 | 近义/替换 |
|------|-----------|
| perpetuate | keep alive / continue |
| blight | pollute / spoil |
| moral licensing | self-congratulation that blocks further action |
| systemic | structural / fundamental |
| hold to account | demand accountability from |
| rein in | curb / restrain |
