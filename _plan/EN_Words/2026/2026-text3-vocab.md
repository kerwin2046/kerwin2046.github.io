## 第 1–2 段

| 单词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 原文语境 |
|-----------|------|------|----------|
| **preferred means** | phr. | 首选方式 | the preferred means of travel |
| **maintenance works** | phr. | 维护工程 | numerous maintenance works |
| **throw into chaos** | phr. | 陷入混乱 | thrown the rail industry into chaos |
| **account for** | phr. | 占（比例） | tourism accounts for 10 percent of GDP |

---

## 第 3–4 段

| 单词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 原文语境 |
|-----------|------|------|----------|
| **disruption** | n. | 中断；延误 | Disruptions have become increasingly frequent |
| **fixture** | n. | 固定存在的事物 | delays now a fixture in Italian media |
| **publicly controlled** | phr. | 公有/国家控股 | the publicly controlled group |
| **obsolete** | adj. | 过时的；陈旧的 | Italy's obsolete infrastructure |
| **beneficiary** | n. | 受益方 | largest European recovery fund beneficiary |

## 第 5–7 段

| 单词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 原文语境 |
|-----------|------|------|----------|
| **lack of capacity** | phr. | 运力不足 | lack of capacity is another problem |
| **spare capacity** | phr. | 备用运力 | there is no spare capacity |

| **traffic fluidity** | phr. | 交通流畅度 | increase in capacity and traffic fluidity |
