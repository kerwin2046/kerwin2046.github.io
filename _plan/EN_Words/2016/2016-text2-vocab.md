## 第 1 段

| 单词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 原文语境 |
|-----------|------|------|----------|
| **estimate** | v. | 估计 | Biologists estimate that… |
| **stretching grasslands** | phr. | 延伸的草原 | living on stretching grasslands |
| **lent red to** | phr. | 为……添一抹红（喻） | once lent red to the often grey landscape |
| **historic range** | phr. | 历史分布范围 | 16% of the species’ historic range |

## 易混 / 同义替换

| 原文 | 近义/替换 |
|------|-----------|
| threatened vs endangered | 受威胁（较轻）/ 濒危（更重） |
| crack down | clamp down / take tough action |
| confrontational | adversarial / combative |
| compensate | reimburse / pay back |
| win-win rhetoric | mutually beneficial talk |
| in the driver’s seat | in control / calling the shots |
