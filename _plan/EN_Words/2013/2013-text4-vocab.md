## 易混 / 同义替换

| 原文 | 近义/替换 |
|------|-----------|
| compel | force / require |
| voluntary vs legally binding | optional vs mandatory |
| glass ceiling | invisible barrier to advancement |
| meritocracy | rule by merit |
| run counter to | contradict |
| evade | avoid / skirt |
