> 主题：马德里清洁空气区反复——城市治污的局限与汽车业责任。

---

|---------|------|

| roll out | 推出 |

| overrule | 推翻（裁决） |
| patchwork | 拼凑式 |
| city-by-city | 逐城推进 |
| politically controversial | 政治上有争议的 |
| vulnerable | 脆弱的 |

---

## 第 1 段

| 单词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 原文语境 |
|-----------|------|------|----------|
| **hailed as** | phr. | 被誉为 | Madrid was hailed as a public health guiding light |
| **roll out** | phr. | 推出 | rolled out ambitious restrictions |
| **suspend enforcement** | phr. | 暂停执行 | suspended enforcement of the clean air zone |
| **termination** | n. | 终止 | toward its possible termination |
| **centrepiece** | n. | 核心议题 | made opposition to the zone a centrepiece |
| **overrule** | v. | 推翻 | A judge has now overruled the city's decision |
| **levy fines** | phr. | 征收罚款 | stop levying fines |

---

## 第 2 段

| 单词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 原文语境 |
|-----------|------|------|----------|
| **back and forth** | phr. | 反复摇摆 | Madrid's back and forth on clean air |
| **patchwork** | n. | 拼凑式 | the patchwork, city-by-city approach |
| **characterise** | v. | 以……为特征 | that characterises efforts on air pollution |

| **vulnerable** | adj. | 脆弱的 | and therefore vulnerable |

## 易混 / 同义替换

| 原文 | 近义/替换 |
|------|-----------|
| hailed as | praised as / celebrated as |
| roll out | launch / introduce |
| suspend enforcement | halt implementation |

| vulnerable | at risk / fragile |
