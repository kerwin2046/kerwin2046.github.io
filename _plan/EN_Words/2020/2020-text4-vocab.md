## 第 1 段

| 单词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 原文语境 |
|-----------|------|------|----------|
| **Generation Z** | phr. | Z 世代 | members of Generation Z |
| **labor market** | phr. | 劳动力市场 | looking for work in a labor market |
| **tighter than** | phr. | 比……更紧俏 | tighter than it's been in decades |
| **inhabit** | v. | 占据 | will soon inhabit those empty office cubicles |
| **cubicle** | n. | 隔间工位 | empty office cubicles |
| **differ from** | phr. | 与……不同 | how … will differ from those who came before |
