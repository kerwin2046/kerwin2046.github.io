# 2020 考研英语（二）Text 2 — 中难点词汇

> 主题：CEO 薪酬上涨——稀缺人才、全球化与治理趋严并存。

---

| 词/短语 | 释义 |
|---------|------|
| publicly traded | 上市的 |
| by varying estimates | 据不同估算 |
| dynamic element | 活力要素 |

| financial markets | 金融市场 |
| slip up | 失误 |
| public relations | 公共关系 |

---

## 第 1–2 段

| 单词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 原文语境 |
|-----------|------|------|----------|
| **publicly traded** | adj. | 上市的 | large publicly traded American corporations |
| **by varying estimates** | phr. | 据不同估算 | by varying estimates, gone up by about 500% |
| **dynamic element** | phr. | 活力要素 | one of the more dynamic elements |
| **upped their game** | phr. | 提升了表现 | CEOs really have upped their game |
| **limited talent** | phr. | 稀缺人才 | limited CEO talent |

---

## 第 3 段

| 单词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 原文语境 |
|-----------|------|------|----------|
| **financial markets** | phr. | 金融市场 | a good sense of financial markets |
| **trade in** | phr. | 在……中交易 | how the company should trade in them |
| **public relations** | phr. | 公共关系 | better public relations skills |
| **slip up** | phr. | 失误 | even a minor slip up |
| **globalized** | adj. | 全球化的 | much more globalized than ever before |
| **supply chain** | phr. | 供应链 | supply chains spread across … countries |
| **mind-boggling** | adj. | 令人难以置信的 | knowledge that is fairly mind-boggling |

---

## 第 4–5 段

| 单词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 原文语境 |
|-----------|------|------|----------|
| **rip people off** | phr. | 坑人 | mainly about ripping people off |
| **corporate governance** | phr. | 公司治理 | corporate governance has become … tighter |
| **rigorous** | adj. | 严格的 | more rigorous since the 1970s |
| **recruit** | v. | 招募 | in the broader corporate interest to recruit top candidates |
| **outside candidates** | phr. | 外部候选人 | paid to outside candidates |
| **cozy insider** | phr. | 圈内亲信 | not to the cozy insider picks |
| **stock price** | phr. | 股价 | tie CEO pay to … stock prices |
| **corporate value** | phr. | 公司价值 | practices build up corporate value |

---

## 易混 / 同义替换

| 原文 | 近义/替换 |
|------|-----------|
| upped their game | improved performance |
| slip up | mistake / blunder |
| mind-boggling | astonishing / overwhelming |
| rip people off | exploit / cheat |
| rigorous | strict / demanding |
| cozy insider | internal favorite |
