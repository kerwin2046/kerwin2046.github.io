| 单词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 原文语境 |
|-----------|------|------|----------|
| **biotech** | n. | 生物技术 | America's biotech industry |
| **patent** | n./v. | 专利 | won patents for isolated DNA |
| **isolated DNA** | phr. | 分离出的 DNA | patents for isolated DNA |
| **unpatentable** | adj. | 不可专利的 | genes were unpatentable |
| **agitated** | adj. | 激动的；不安的 | violently agitated |
| **trade group** | phr. | 行业团体 | a trade group |
| **preliminary step** | phr. | 初步步骤 | just a "preliminary step" |
| **overturn** | v. | 推翻 | overturned the prior decision |
| **forecast** | v. | 预测 | help forecast a woman's risk |

## 易混 / 同义替换

| 原文 | 近义/替换 |
|------|-----------|
| unpatentable | not eligible for patent |
| overturn | reverse / overrule |
| suppress innovation | stifle innovation |
| product of nature | naturally occurring |
| public domain | freely available |
| restrict access | limit availability |
