## 第 1 段

| 单词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 原文语境 |
|-----------|------|------|----------|
| **Great Recession** | 专名 | 大衰退 | The Great Recession may be over |
| **joblessness** | n. | 失业状态 | era of high joblessness |
| **life course** | phr. | 人生轨迹 | change the life course |
| **reshape** | v. | 重塑 | reshape our politics, our culture |
