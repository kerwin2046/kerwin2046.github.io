# 2023 考研英语（二）Text 4 — 中难点词汇

---

| 词/短语 | 释义 |
|---------|------|
| paradoxical | 矛盾的 |
| prosocial | 亲社会的 |
| rebelliousness | 叛逆 |

---

## 第 1 段

| 单词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 原文语境 |
|-----------|------|------|----------|
| **paradoxical** | adj. | 矛盾的 | Teenagers are paradoxical |
| **helpless and dependent** | phr. | 无助且依赖 | helpless and dependent children |
| **risk-takers** | n. | 冒险者 | rebellious teenage risk-takers |

---

## 第 2 段

| 单词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 原文语境 |
|-----------|------|------|----------|
| **go hand in hand** | phr. | 相伴发生 | positive and negative sides… go hand in hand |
| **opportunity as well as risk** | phr. | 机遇与风险并存 | time of opportunity as well as risk |

---

## 第 3–4 段

| 单词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 原文语境 |
|-----------|------|------|----------|
| **prosocial** | adj. | 亲社会的 | studied "prosocial" and rebellious traits |
| **altruistic** | adj. | 利他的 | things that were altruistic and positive |
| **sacrificing their own interests** | phr. | 牺牲自身利益 | sacrificing their own interests to help a friend |
| **positive correlation** | phr. | 正相关 | positive correlation between prosociality and rebelliousness |

## 第 5–6 段

| 单词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 原文语境 |
|-----------|------|------|----------|
