# 2022 考研英语（二）Text 2 — 中难点词汇

> 主题：“不退休”与退休后继续工作

---

## 速查总表

| 词/短语 | 释义 |
|---------|------|
| upend | 颠覆 |
| unretirees | 不退休者 |
| personal fulfillment | 自我实现 |
| evolving | 演变中的 |
| life spans | 寿命 |
| nest eggs | 养老积蓄 |
| making ends meet | 收支相抵 |
| preretirees | 准退休人群 |

---

## 第 1 段

| 单词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 原文语境 |
|-----------|------|------|----------|
| **opt to** | phr. | 选择做 | opting to work well into retirement |
| **upend** | v. | 颠覆；打乱 | threatens to upend the old workforce model |

---

## 第 2 段

| 单词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 原文语境 |
|-----------|------|------|----------|
| **unretirees** | n. | 不退休者；退休后仍工作者 | more than half of "unretirees" |
| **settle down** | phr. | 安顿下来；退休安顿 | enough money to settle down |

---

## 第 3–4 段

| 单词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 原文语境 |
|-----------|------|------|----------|
| **culprit** | n. | 原因；罪魁祸首 | Financial needs aren't the only culprit |
| **personal fulfillment** | phr. | 自我实现 | personal fulfillment such as… |
| **mentally fit** | phr. | 保持思维活跃 | staying mentally fit |
| **evolving** | adj. | 演变中的 | retirement is evolving |
| **driving folks** | phr. | 促使人们 | driving folks to continue working |

---

## 第 5–6 段

| 单词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 原文语境 |
|-----------|------|------|----------|
| **life spans** | phr. | 寿命 | Because of longer life spans |
| **fastest-growing segment** | phr. | 增长最快的群体 | fastest-growing segment of the workforce |
| **nest eggs** | phr. | 养老积蓄 | preserve their nest eggs |
| **in anticipation of** | phr. | 预料；为……做准备 | in anticipation of a longer life |

---

## 第 7–8 段

| 单词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 原文语境 |
|-----------|------|------|----------|
| **making ends meet** | phr. | 收支相抵；糊口 | worried about making ends meet |
| **preretirees** | n. | 准退休人群 | preretirees should speak with… |
| **financial adviser** | phr. | 财务顾问 | speak with a financial adviser |

---

## 易混 / 同义替换

| 原文 | 近义/替换 |
|------|-----------|
| upend | overturn / disrupt |
| unretirees | those who keep working after retiring |
| nest eggs | retirement savings |
| making ends meet | getting by financially |
