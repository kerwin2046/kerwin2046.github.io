# 2022 考研英语（二）Text 1 — 中难点词汇

> 主题：再生农业与“气候友好型”鸡蛋

---

## 速查总表

| 词/短语 | 释义 |
|---------|------|
| windswept | 风吹日晒的 |
| by design | 有意为之 |
| regenerative agriculture | 再生农业 |
| trap greenhouse gases | 固存温室气体 |
| forage | 觅食 |
| cover crops | 覆盖作物 |
| premium offering | 高端品类 |
| open the floodgates | 打开闸门（喻大潮） |
| hard sell | 难推销 |
| chalk up to | 归因于 |

---

## 第 1 段

| 单词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 原文语境 |
|-----------|------|------|----------|
| **windswept** | adj. | 风吹日晒的 | 40 windswept acres |
| **roam** | v. | 漫游；自由活动 | chickens roam over… acres |
| **by design** | phr. | 有意为之；按设计 | random, but it's by design |
| **climate-friendly** | adj. | 气候友好的 | climate-friendly eggs |
| **greenhouse gases** | phr. | 温室气体 | trap greenhouse gases |

---

## 第 2–3 段

| 单词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 原文语境 |
|-----------|------|------|----------|
| **regenerative agriculture** | phr. | 再生农业 | using regenerative agriculture |
| **cultivate rich soils** | phr. | 培育肥沃土壤 | cultivate rich soils |
| **forage** | v. | 觅食 | hens to forage for… feed |
| **cover crops** | phr. | 覆盖作物 | adding more cover crops |
| **fertilize** | v. | 施肥；使肥沃 | waste then fertilizes fields |

---

## 第 4–5 段

| 单词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 原文语境 |
|-----------|------|------|----------|
| **premium offering** | phr. | 高端品类 | next premium offering |
| **niche product** | phr. | 小众产品 | dismissed as a niche product |
| **plant-based meats** | phr. | 植物肉 | probiotics and plant-based meats |
| **open the floodgates** | phr. | 打开闸门（喻大规模涌入） | open the floodgates for regenerative beef |
| **hard sell** | phr. | 难推销 | could be a hard sell |
| **tough to define** | phr. | 难以快速界定 | concept is tough to define quickly |

---

## 第 6 段

| 单词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 原文语境 |
|-----------|------|------|----------|
| **free-range** | adj. | 散养的 | attributes such as free-range |
| **non-GMO** | adj. | 非转基因的 | non-GMO |
| **pasture-raised** | adj. | 牧场饲养的 | pasture-raised eggs |
| **embrace sustainability** | phr. | 接受可持续理念 | will embrace sustainability |
| **chalk up to** | phr. | 归因于 | chalked up to shoppers wanting… |

## 易混 / 同义替换

| 原文 | 近义/替换 |
|------|-----------|
| regenerative | restorative / sustainable |
| trap gases | sequester carbon |
| open the floodgates | unleash a wave of |
| hard sell | tough to market |
| chalk up to | attribute to |
