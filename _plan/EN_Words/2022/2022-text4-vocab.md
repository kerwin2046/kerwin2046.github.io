# 2022 考研英语（二）Text 4 — 中难点词汇

> 主题：伦理课能否改变吃肉行为

---

## 速查总表

| 词/短语 | 释义 |
|---------|------|
| contrived | 人为设计的 |
| self-reports | 自我报告 |
| factory-farmed | 工厂化养殖的 |
| anonymized | 匿名的 |
| confounding variable | 混淆变量 |
| nudge | 助推 |
| social influence | 社会影响 |

---

## 第 1 段

| 单词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 原文语境 |
|-----------|------|------|----------|
| **contrived** | adj. | 人为设计的；不自然的 | contrived laboratory tests |
| **self-reports** | phr. | 自我报告 | unreliable self-reports |
| **lasting effects** | phr. | 持久影响 | may have had lasting effects |

---

## 第 2 段

| 单词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 原文语境 |
|-----------|------|------|----------|
| **factory-farmed meat** | phr. | 工厂化养殖肉类 | ethics of factory-farmed meat |
| **anonymized** | adj. | 匿名的 | studied their anonymized meal-card purchases |
| **charitable giving** | phr. | 慈善捐赠 | focused on charitable giving instead |

---

## 第 3–4 段

| 单词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 原文语境 |
|-----------|------|------|----------|
| **intervention** | n. | 干预 | the intervention would have no effect |
| **ethics professors** | phr. | 伦理学教授 | ethics professors do not differ… |
| **held steady** | phr. | 保持稳定 | this effect held steady |

---

## 第 5–6 段

| 单词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 原文语境 |
|-----------|------|------|----------|
| **confounding variable** | phr. | 混淆变量 | some unknown confounding variable |
| **nudge** | n. | 助推；轻微推动 | reversible by another nudge |
| **social influence** | phr. | 社会影响 | greatest impact came from social influence |
| **vegetarianism** | n. | 素食主义 | shared their own vegetarianism |
| **rational argument** | phr. | 理性论证 | Least rousing… was rational argument |
| **eating his words** | phr. | 承认自己说错（字面：食言） | will be eating his words |

---

## 易混 / 同义替换

| 原文 | 近义/替换 |
|------|-----------|
| contrived | artificial / staged |
| anonymized | de-identified |
| confounding variable | hidden factor |
| nudge | gentle push / behavioral cue |

---

## 复习小测

1. contrived / self-reports  
2. factory-farmed  
3. anonymized  
4. confounding variable  
5. nudge  
6. social influence
