## 易混 / 同义替换（做题常考）

| 原文表达 | 近义/替换方向 |
|----------|----------------|
| bull run ended | market boom ended / boom came to an end |
| losing momentum | slowing down / weakening |
| downturn / slump | decline / fall / recession |
| unfashionable | unpopular / out of favor |
| overheated | inflated / too hot |
| at the bottom | hit bottom / reached the lowest point |
| keep away | stay out of the market / hold back |
