---

## 第 1–2 段

| 单词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 原文语境 |
|-----------|------|------|----------|
| **fossil fuels** | phr. | 化石燃料 | While fossil fuels… still generate… |
| **renewable** | adj. | 可再生的 | the future belongs to renewable sources |
| **picking up momentum** | phr. | 势头增强 | The move to renewables is picking up momentum |
| **going on line** | phr. | 并网；上线 | new power sources going on line |
| **farsighted** | adj. | 有远见的 | governments and farsighted businesses |
| **plummeting prices** | phr. | 价格骤降 | the plummeting prices of renewables |

---

## 第 3–4 段

| 单词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 原文语境 |
|-----------|------|------|----------|
| **principal energy source** | phr. | 主要能源 | already a principal energy source |
| **wind turbines** | phr. | 风力涡轮机 | wind turbines provide enough electricity |
| **remarkable shift** | phr. | 显著转变 | the United States is also seeing a remarkable shift |
| **accounted for** | phr. | 占（比例） | wind and solar power accounted for more than 10 percent |
| **underlined** | v. | 强调；划线突出 | Trump has underlined fossil fuels |
| **dismissed as unreliable** | phr. | 斥为不可靠 | dismissed wind power as an unreliable energy source |
| **dot the fields** | phr. | 遍布田野 | wind turbines dot the fields |

|-----------|------|------|----------|

## 易混 / 同义替换

| 原文 | 近义/替换 |
|------|-----------|
| picking up momentum | gaining speed |
| plummeting | plunging / nosediving |
| principal | main / primary |
| remarkable shift | significant change |
| put-down | rebuttal / dismissive reply |
| trend lines spiking | sharp upward trend |
