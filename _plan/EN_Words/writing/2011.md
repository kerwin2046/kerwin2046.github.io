# 2011 考研英语（二）写作

## Part A 小作文（约 100 词）
Direction
Suppose your cousin, Liming, has just been admitted to a university, write him/her a letter to
congratulate him/her, and
give him/her suggestions on how to get prepared for university life.
1. 语言特点：选文话题贴近现代生活，语言表达总体直白。考生需注意处理文中包含的复合句与长句。
Your should write about 100 words on ANSER SHEET 2.
Do not sign your own name at the end of the letter. Use “Zhang Wei” instead.
Do not write the address. (10 points)

### 参考示例
Dear Li Ming,

I am thrilled to hear about your admission to a prestigious university. Congratulations on this remarkable achievement! Your hard work has truly paid off.

As you prepare for this new chapter, I’d like to offer some suggestions. Firstly, embrace independence by learning to manage your time and daily affairs. Secondly, cultivate self-learning habits, as university studies require more proactive exploration beyond textbooks. Lastly, get involved in campus activities to broaden your horizons and make new friends.

Wishing you a fulfilling and successful university journey!

Yours, Zhang Wei

三、【思路框架】

1. 首段（祝贺）：直接表达喜悦之情，并对对方的成就表示祝贺，肯定其付出的努力。
2. 次段（建议）：自然过渡到大学生活准备，提出具体、实用的建议。建议应围绕新生常见的挑战展开，如培养独立性、转变学习方式、融入集体等。
3. 结尾（祝愿）：以美好的祝愿结束全文，简洁有力。

四、【写作储备】

大学新生待培养的素质

- 学习方面：self-learning ability (自学能力), independent thinking (独立思考)
- 生活方面：time-management skills (时间管理能力), self-discipline (自律能力), independence (独立性)
- 个人发展：communication skills (沟通能力), adaptability (适应能力), a sense of responsibility (责任感)

祝贺信常用表达

开头语:

- I am so pleased/glad/delighted to hear that… (得知…我非常高兴。)
- Please accept my warmest/heartiest congratulations on… (请接受我对…最热烈的祝贺。)

发展部分:

- You’ve deserved this success after all your hard work. (经过所有努力，你值得拥有这份成功。)
- This is a significant milestone in your life. (这是你人生中的一个重要里程碑。)

结束语:

- Wishing you all the best in your future studies and life at university. (祝你在大学未来的学习和生活一切顺利。)
- May you have a brilliant and fruitful university experience. (愿你拥有精彩而充实的大学经历。)
