# 2019 考研英语（二）写作

## Part A 小作文（约 100 词）
Suppose professor Smith asked you to plan a debate on the theme of city traffic. Write him an email to
suggest a specific topic with your reasons, and
tell him your arrangement.
You should write about 100 words on the ANSWER SHEET.
Do not use your own name. Use “Li Ming” instead.
Do not write your address. (10 points)

### 参考示例
Dear Professor Smith,

Thank you for trusting me to arrange the debate. As the competition is nearly ready, I am writing to suggest a topic and outline the plan.

I propose the topic: “Is Traffic Jam Primarily Caused by Inadequate Transport Infrastructure or Poor Management?” This topic is highly debatable, touching on key urban issues. It also connects with course content on urban planning and public policy, helping students deepen their understanding. Additionally, it involves current concerns like shared bikes, which can stimulate active participation.

The debate will be held in the school hall this Friday, from 6:00 p.m. to 8:00 p.m. I look forward to your feedback.

Yours sincerely,Li Ming

Write an essay based on the chart below. In your writing, you should
interpret the chart, and
give your comments.
You should write about 150 words on the ANSWER SHEET. (15 points)
