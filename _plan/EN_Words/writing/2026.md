# 2026 考研英语（二）写作

## Part A 小作文（约 100 词）
Directions: Suppose your friend Jack has shared with you a video of his family’s travel in China. Write an email to tell him
(1) how you feel about the video, and(2) your wish to learn more about their travel.
Write your answer in about 100 words on the ANSWER SHEET.
Do not use your own name in your email; Use “Li Ming” instead. (10 points)

### 参考示例
Dear Jack,

I just watched the video of your family’s trip to China, and I was truly captivated by the stunning landscapes and vibrant local cultures you captured. It felt as if I were traveling alongside you all!

I would love to hear more about your experiences, especially the stories behind those lively street markets and ancient temples. Would you mind sharing more details or photos when you have time?

Looking forward to hearing from you!

Best regards,Li Ming

## Part B 大作文（约 150 词）
Directions: Write an essay based on the chart below. In your essay, you should
1. describe and interpret the chart, and
2. give your comments.
You should write about 150 words on the ANSWER SHEET. (15 points)

**图表说明**：柱状图——家长对儿童户外活动益处的看法：满足好奇心 54.6%；促进观察力 54.5%；强身健体 37.2%；增强亲子互动 33.2%（最低）。

### 参考范文
The bar chart presents a survey on parents’ views regarding the benefits of children’s outdoor activities. Among the four listed advantages, “satisfying curiosity” ranks first at 54.6%, followed closely by “enhancing observation skills” at 54.5%. In contrast, “improving physical health” accounts for 37.2%, while “strengthening parent-child interaction” is the least selected at 33.2%.

The data clearly reflect contemporary parenting priorities. The top two choices highlight cognitive and intellectual development, suggesting that parents increasingly value outdoor exploration as a means to stimulate children’s minds. Meanwhile, the lower percentages for physical health and family bonding may imply that these aspects, though recognized, are considered secondary or are pursued through other avenues. This trend mirrors a broader educational shift towards holistic child development, where mental growth is emphasized alongside traditional concerns.
