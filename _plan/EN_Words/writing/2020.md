# 2020 考研英语（二）写作

## Part A 小作文（约 100 词）
Suppose you are planning a tour of a historical site for a group of international students. write an email to
tell them about the site, and
give them some tips for the tour
Please write your answer on the ANSWER SHEET.
Do not use your own name, use Li Ming instead (10 points)

### 参考示例
Dear Friends,

I’m so delighted that you trust me to arrange your trip. I’m writing to inform you of the historical attraction we will visit—the Terracotta Army, one of the greatest archaeological discoveries of all time—and to share some travel tips with you.

Located to the east of downtown Xi’an, the Terracotta Army is a massive collection of life-size clay warriors and horses buried in the tomb of China’s First Emperor. It offers a fascinating glimpse into the country’s glorious past.

To help you better appreciate these breathtaking sculptures, I suggest avoiding Chinese public holidays if possible. If you must visit on those days, it’s best to set out early. Additionally, I recommend renting a multilingual audio guide at the ticket office, which will surely enrich your experience.

I hope this trip suits your taste and that you all have a wonderful time.

Yours sincerely,Li Ming
