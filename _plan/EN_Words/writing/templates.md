# 考研英语（二）写作 — 通用模板与句式

> 小作文 10 分 / 大作文 15 分。模板需**背框架、练替换**，考场忌全文照搬。

---

## 一、书信 / 邮件格式

```
Dear XXX,

[开头 1 句：目的/感谢/回应]

[主体：分点 1–2，每点 2–3 句]

[结尾 1–2 句：期待/感谢/祝愿]

Best regards,
Li Ming
```

**禁止**：真实姓名、地址（题干要求 Use "Li Ming"）。

---

## 二、小作文 Part A — 类型与开头

| 类型 | 开头句型 |
|------|----------|
| 建议信 | I am writing to offer some suggestions regarding… |
| 道歉信 | I am writing to apologize for… |
| 邀请信 | I am writing to invite you to… |
| 祝贺信 | I am writing to extend my heartfelt congratulations on… |
| 投诉信 | I am writing to express my dissatisfaction with… |
| 感谢信 | I am writing to express my sincere gratitude for… |
| 通知/邮件（活动） | I am writing to inform you about… / to encourage you to participate in… |

**常用衔接**：First, … Second, … / Moreover, … / I would appreciate it if you could…

**结尾**：I would be grateful if you could consider my suggestions. / Looking forward to your reply.

## 三、大作文 Part B — 两段式结构

### 图表类

**第 1 段（描述，约 60–70 词）**

- The chart/graph illustrates …
- According to the data, … accounts for the largest share at X%, followed by … at Y%.
- In contrast, … ranks the lowest at Z%.

**第 2 段（评论，约 80–90 词）**

- The figures reflect / suggest that …
- Several factors may contribute to this phenomenon.
- In my view, … is of great significance.
- It is advisable for … to …

### 图画 / 观点类

**第 1 段**：描述图画 + 点明寓意（The cartoon depicts … / The message is clear that …）

**第 2 段**：分析原因或意义 + 建议（This phenomenon can be attributed to … / We should take measures to …）

## 四、高分替换词（择记）

| 基础 | 升级 |
|------|------|
| important | crucial / vital / of great significance |
| more and more | an increasing number of / a growing proportion of |
| think | argue / maintain / hold the view that |
| because | owing to / due to / on account of |
| so | therefore / consequently / hence |
| good | beneficial / favorable / constructive |
| bad | detrimental / adverse / unfavorable |
